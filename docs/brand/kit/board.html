The brief

Android app that replaces the shop's paper ledger: record purchases and sales by voice, barcode or by hand; stock, invoices and reports. For supermarket, grocery and nut-shop owners who are not accountants.

simpletrustworthywarmfastIranian

Concept A

Tally — چوب‌خط

Four tally strokes — the oldest way to count stock — closed by a check mark instead of a slash: counted and recorded.

  • Says the product's promise, 'it's recorded', without drawing a cart or a ledger
  • The strokes double as goods side by side on a shelf
  • The wordmark's alefs end in the same round caps as the strokes
  • True negative-space gutter: works on cream, olive, black and white
